# Product Guidelines

## Design Language & Visual Style
- **Minimalist & High Contrast:** The application features a clean user interface with large, easily readable text, optimized for quick glances during intense workout sessions.

## Accessibility
- **Standard Compliance:** We adhere to standard React Native accessibility practices to ensure basic screen reader support (VoiceOver/TalkBack) and a broad level of usability for all users.

## Tone & Voice
- **Professional & Direct:** All user-facing copy, notifications, and prompts should be professional and direct. (e.g., "Workout completed. 5 minutes recorded.")
